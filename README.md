# 🧋 TapiokaNeko 🐱

> **An anti-burnout desktop focus companion & virtual pet housed inside a custom 3D-printed Boba Tea cup.**

---

## 🌟 Overview

**TapiokaNeko** is a physical, interactive desktop companion designed to help students prevent burnout and maintain healthy study habits. Built for the Hack Club *Fallen* program, TapiokaNeko combines a **Pomodoro focus timer** with a **virtual pet care loop**.

* **Focus Sessions:** Tapioka sips boba and stays happy on screen while you work!
* **Pet Care Loop:** Feed, play, and take care of Tapioka using three physical tactile buttons.
* **Tea Mood Lighting:** Ambient RGB glow inside the cup reflects study modes and pet health.

---

## 🛠️ Hardware & Components (BOM)

* **Brain:** Raspberry Pi Pico (or ESP32)
* **Display:** 0.96" OLED Display (128x64 pixels)
* **Lighting:** Neopixel / WS2812B RGB LED Ring
* **Inputs:** 3x Tactile Push Buttons (Custom 3D-printed Boba Pearl caps)
* **Audio:** 5V Passive Piezo Buzzer
* **Enclosure:** Custom 3D-Printed Boba Tea Cup with Cat Ears & Straw Lanyard Loop
